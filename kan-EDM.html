Important update to our summer camp schedule

Crossings Parents,

We want to share an important update to our summer camp schedule. After prayerful consideration, Crossings has decided not to host Kanakuk KampOut at any of our locations this summer. Instead, we are encouraging our families to participate in other camps we offer throughout the summer.

We know this news may come as a disappointment, especially for families and kids who were looking forward to KampOut. If your child is already enrolled, here’s what you need to know to receive a refund:

To receive a full refund, all cancellations must be submitted in writing or e-mailed to reg@kanakuk.com. Any event cancelled before May 15 will receive a full refund of tuition.

After May 15 and leading up to 7 days before your KampOut event, cancellation will result in a forfeit of a $100 deposit.

What else can I get my kids involved in?

As always, Crossings will have activities designed to help your kids grow in their faith all summer long. While plans may be changing, there are still many opportunities ahead, and we’re excited about what’s coming this summer.

Be on the lookout for Crossings Kids activities this summer, including Kids Camp (3rd–6th grade), Musical Theater Camp (2nd–8th grade), and other family-friendly events. Summer Camp registrations will be available online beginning February 15.

You can find information about activities for kids and families as it becomes available at crossings.church/info.

We are sorry for any inconvenience and thank you for your patience and understanding. If you have any questions, please feel free to reach out to me.

Thank you,
